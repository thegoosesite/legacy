Frequently Asked Questions

These are questions that our developers get all the time and we are DONE answering them all so we put up a page.

Is Goosism a real religion/cult?

No. This was a joke started in a coding class, and has since turned into this monstrosity of a "religion".

How I can support this website?

Due to The Goose Site being classified as free and open source software, we currently do not have a donation system. Thank you for your interest!

I can't access the page because I don't have the passcode. What is it?

Please Contact Us via email for the password, though we may not give it to you if you do not meet gooseling criteria.

Is The Goose Site in beta?

This is an extremely common misconception. We are not in beta, we protect our site from non-gooselings since not everyone is comfortable with worshipping a pseudocult.

Why did the site's domain name move?

Some gooselings had a hard time trying to find our site since it was called "worshipthegoose.github.io" and Google's webcrawler blacklisted our domain for being cultish. So, our devs changed it to the one you see today!
